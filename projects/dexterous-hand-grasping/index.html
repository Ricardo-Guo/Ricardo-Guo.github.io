Project case study

Vision-Based Dexterous-Hand Grasping with Reinforcement Learning

A five-finger dexterous-hand system that learns vision-guided grasping policies in Isaac Gym and transfers them to embedded hardware.

Reinforcement LearningIsaac GymRGB-DPyTorchJetson

Project Overview

This project explores vision-guided robotic grasping with a five-finger dexterous hand and a reconfigurable palm. The goal is to connect mechanical hand design, RGB-D perception, reinforcement-learning simulation, and physical motor control in one end-to-end system.

Working with four teammates, I helped establish a training pipeline based on UniDexGrasp++ and prepared the perception and embedded-control components needed for physical validation.

What I Built

  • Contributed to the concept of a five-finger hand with a reconfigurable palm surface.
  • Built an Isaac Gym simulation environment for grasp-policy training.
  • Processed RGB-D observations and back-projected depth maps into point-cloud inputs.
  • Debugged an Arduino-based multi-motor control interface.
  • Deployed trained policies on Jetson Nano Super for physical-system validation.

System Pipeline

The workflow begins with RGB-D observations, converts depth measurements into a spatial point representation, and feeds that representation into the learned grasping policy. Actions produced by the policy are then mapped to the hand’s motor-control interface for simulation and hardware testing.

Technical Stack

Area Tools / Methods
