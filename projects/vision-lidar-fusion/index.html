Project case study

Vision-LiDAR Fusion for 3D Target Localization

A synchronized perception pipeline that combines YOLO detections with Livox point clouds for accurate 3D localization and motion estimation.

YOLOLivox LiDAROpenCVNVIDIA JetsonSensor Fusion

Project Overview

This project develops a vision-LiDAR fusion pipeline for locating spherical targets in 3D and estimating their motion. It combines image-space object detection with projected LiDAR measurements, turning complementary camera and depth observations into one spatial estimate.

The system is designed for real-time robotic perception rather than offline analysis. A global-shutter camera and Livox LiDAR are hardware-synchronized, while the processing pipeline runs and is tested on NVIDIA Jetson hardware.

What I Built

  • Integrated YOLO-based target detection with LiDAR point-cloud projection.
  • Implemented point-cloud clustering and 2D-3D association between image detections and LiDAR returns.
  • Hardware-synchronized a global-shutter camera and Livox LiDAR.
  • Built a motion-estimation stage from consecutive 3D target observations.
  • Validated localization results against motion-capture ground truth.

Results

For targets within 5 metres, 95% of detections achieved a 3D localization error below 10 cm when evaluated against motion-capture measurements.
