Project case study

Metamorphic-Structure Reconfigurable Aircraft

A long-endurance aircraft prototype that transforms between airship and fixed-wing configurations through an integrated mechanical and control system.

ROS 2GazeboPixhawkSTM32SolidWorks

Project Overview

This project investigates a metamorphic aircraft for long-endurance, low-altitude exploration. The vehicle switches between an airship configuration and a twin-rotor fixed-wing configuration, using a cable-driven winding mechanism to open and close the aircraft frame.

The prototype demonstrates how mechanical transformation, flight-control hardware, and embedded actuation can be integrated into one platform. The work received RMB 20,000 in research funding through the SUSTech Climbing Program.

Transformation Concept

The central mechanism uses a winding assembly to coordinate the opening and closing of the airframe. In its expanded state, the structure supports the fixed-wing flight configuration; after folding, it transitions toward the compact airship configuration. The design focuses on proving the mechanical and control concept at prototype scale.

Design Development

The project progressed from structural drawings and mechanism studies to a physical prototype. Iterating between CAD design and hardware assembly helped expose constraints around cable routing, frame synchronization, actuation range, and repeatable configuration switching.
