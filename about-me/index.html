Research Experience

Vision-LiDAR Fusion for 3D Target Localization

Built a synchronized perception pipeline combining YOLO, Livox point-cloud projection, clustering, and 2D-3D association. Validation against motion-capture ground truth showed that 95% of detections within 5 m had 3D localization error below 10 cm.

Vision-Based Dexterous-Hand Grasping

Built an Isaac Gym and UniDexGrasp++-based grasp-training pipeline, processed RGB-D observations into point-cloud inputs, debugged Arduino multi-motor control, and deployed trained policies on Jetson Nano Super.

Metamorphic-Structure Reconfigurable Aircraft

Participated in the mechanical and control design of an aircraft that switches between airship and fixed-wing configurations. Built the Gazebo/ROS 2 simulation environment and worked with Pixhawk, QGroundControl, STM32, and Arduino.
